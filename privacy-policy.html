This policy explains how Anarat Naturals handles information submitted through this website and when placing an order.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we may collect the details you provide, such as your name, phone number, city, delivery address and selected product/pack. These details are used to process and confirm your order and arrange delivery.

How information is used

  • To receive, verify and fulfil your order.
  • To contact you about delivery, order changes or customer support.
  • To maintain basic order and business records and help prevent misuse.

Service providers

Order information may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, order storage and delivery. Those providers handle information under their own terms and privacy practices.

Payments

Our website currently presents Cash on Delivery ordering. Do not submit card numbers, banking passwords or other payment credentials through our forms or WhatsApp.

Cookies and local storage

The shopping cart may use your browser's local storage to remember items you add to your cart on your device. Your browser settings can be used to clear local storage.

Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect submitted order information. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for order fulfilment, support and legitimate business recordkeeping.

Your questions

For a privacy-related request or question about information submitted with an Anarat Naturals order, contact us on WhatsApp at +92 309 4114121. We may need to verify your identity before discussing an order. For general product or delivery questions, see our FAQs, shipping and returns information or contact page.
