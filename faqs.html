What does a skin brightening cream do?

Brightening skincare is generally formulated to improve the appearance of dullness or uneven-looking skin tone. Cosmetic products cannot change your natural skin colour, and results vary. Use products as directed and wear broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day. Read our guide to choosing a skin brightening cream for practical label-checking tips.

What do Niacinamide and Alpha Arbutin do in skincare?

Niacinamide is commonly used to support the skin barrier and improve the look of uneven tone. Alpha Arbutin is used in skincare products targeting the appearance of dark spots. Anarat Naturals highlights both ingredients; check the packaging for the complete formula.

Should I use sunscreen with a brightening skin cream?

Daily broad-spectrum sunscreen helps protect skin from UV exposure, which can contribute to dark spots and uneven tone. Follow your sunscreen label and reapply as directed, especially when outdoors.

Is the cream suitable for every skin type?

Skin can react differently. Review the ingredient list, patch test first and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Consult a qualified dermatologist if you have concerns.
