How do I use Anarat Naturals Skin Cream?

Follow the directions printed on the product packaging. Apply only as directed, avoid contact with eyes, and patch test before first use.

What size is the product?

The featured product on our website is the 110 g jar.

Which ingredients are highlighted?

The website highlights Niacinamide, Alpha Arbutin and Hyaluronic Acid. Refer to the product label for the complete and current ingredient list.

When will my order arrive?

Dispatch and delivery timing can vary by destination and courier. Our team will confirm the estimated timeline when your order is confirmed.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible. Changes or cancellations can be requested before dispatch; they cannot be guaranteed once an order has shipped.

What if my parcel arrives damaged or incorrect?

Please contact us promptly with your order details and clear photos of the parcel and item so our team can review the issue.

Is the cream suitable for every skin type?

Skin can react differently. Review the ingredient list, patch test first and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Consult a qualified dermatologist if you have concerns.
