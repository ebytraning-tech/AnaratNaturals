Shipping & Returns
in Pakistan.

Ordering Anarat Naturals Skin Cream online? This page explains our current delivery and return guidance for customers across Pakistan. For product details, visit the Anarat Naturals Skin Cream product page.

Cash on Delivery skincare orders across Pakistan

We accept Cash on Delivery orders across Pakistan, subject to courier service availability at the delivery address.

Skincare delivery charges and estimated timing

Delivery charges and the estimated dispatch/delivery timeline are confirmed with you during order confirmation. Timing may vary by city, courier conditions and service availability.

Order changes and cancellations

If you need to update or cancel an order, contact us on WhatsApp as soon as possible. Requests can be made before dispatch; once a parcel has shipped, changes or cancellation may no longer be possible.

Damaged, missing or incorrect items

If your parcel arrives damaged or contains an incorrect item, contact us promptly. Please keep the packaging and share your order details along with clear photos so we can review the issue and advise on the next steps.

Hygiene and opened products

For hygiene and product-safety reasons, opened or used skincare products may not be eligible for return unless the item is faulty, damaged in transit or incorrect. Please contact our support team before sending anything back. Do not return a parcel without receiving instructions.

Need help with an order?

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to update an order or report a parcel issue. Keep your order details and parcel photographs available so our team can review your request.
